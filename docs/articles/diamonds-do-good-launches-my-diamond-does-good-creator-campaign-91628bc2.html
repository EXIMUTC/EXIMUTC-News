Diamonds Do Good launches My Diamond Does Good creator campaign

October 5, 2026 at 6:18 PM ET

Diamonds Do Good has launched the My Diamond Does Good consumer campaign, featuring four fashion and lifestyle creators who will share the stories behind natural diamond jewelry they already own.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC's market view: the campaign focuses on consumer storytelling around natural diamonds. It does not establish the origin, treatment, grading, or value of any individual diamond.
