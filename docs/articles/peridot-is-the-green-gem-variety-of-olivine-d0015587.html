Peridot is the green gem variety of olivine

October 25, 2026 at 6:43 PM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: Peridot is the gem variety of olivine, with magnesium and iron in its composition. Iron is responsible for its characteristic green to yellowish-green color. Unlike a trade label based only on appearance, the name identifies a particular mineral material. Other green gems, including emerald and green garnet, belong to different mineral groups.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: Start with identification of the actual stone, then compare its shade, transparency and cutting. A green appearance alone cannot establish that a gem is peridot, and a photograph cannot provide a mineral identification. Ask for documentation when the identity is uncertain. Evaluate the individual specimen without transferring claims about emerald or green garnet to it.
