Hard topaz can still split along cleavage planes

November 24, 2026 at 8:42 AM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: Topaz has a Mohs hardness of 8, but hardness measures resistance to scratching rather than resistance to breaking. Its basal cleavage allows it to split along a particular direction if struck. Pressure and sudden temperature changes can also cause damage. This is why a hard gemstone can still need careful handling during cutting, setting, cleaning and everyday wear.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: Choose a protective setting for a topaz ring and tell the jeweler the gem's identity before repair or cleaning. Avoid hard knocks and sudden temperature changes. GIA recommends warm, soapy water instead of steam or ultrasonic cleaning. Consider how the piece will be worn; the hardness number alone should not be treated as a promise of resistance to impact.
