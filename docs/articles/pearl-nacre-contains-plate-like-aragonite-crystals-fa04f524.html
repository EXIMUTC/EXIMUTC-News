Pearl nacre contains plate-like aragonite crystals

October 27, 2026 at 10:37 PM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: Nacre is built from plate-like aragonite crystals, a form of calcium carbonate, held together by organic material. Light interacts with the pearl's surface and concentric nacre layers to produce luster. Nacre is therefore part of the structure behind a pearl's appearance, rather than simply a color description. Its condition matters alongside shape, surface and matching in jewelry.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: Inspect the surface and reflections on the actual pearls, including areas near drill holes and settings. Request the pearl type and any enhancement disclosures separately from an assessment of luster. For a pair or strand, compare the pearls together: an attractive single pearl does not establish that the whole set matches. Ask the supplier to explain how nacre quality was assessed.
