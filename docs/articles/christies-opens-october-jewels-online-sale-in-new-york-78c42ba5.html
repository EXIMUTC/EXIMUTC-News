Christie's opens October Jewels Online sale in New York

October 7, 2026 at 9:25 AM ET

Christie's says its New York Jewels Online sale is open October 6–15, 2026, with more than 200 lots including diamonds, colored gemstones and signed jewels. Independent coverage from Rapaport reports that an 8.17-carat D-color, internally flawless round brilliant diamond ring is among the sale highlights and could fetch up to $350,000.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: auction estimates and headline stone specifications are useful market signals, but any purchase decision should still rely on the individual lot's laboratory report, condition, provenance and final sale terms.
