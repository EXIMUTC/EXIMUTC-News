Fluorite gave fluorescence its name

November 27, 2026 at 2:21 AM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: In 1852, George Stokes named fluorescence after fluorspar, the mineral now commonly called fluorite. Fluorescence can make a gem emit visible light under ultraviolet illumination.
