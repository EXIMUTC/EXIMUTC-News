Luster is a key visual quality factor in pearls

November 21, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: Pearl luster describes the brightness and sharpness of reflected light. Strong luster produces clear, bright reflections; weaker luster looks more diffuse or hazy. It is assessed together with size, shape, color, surface and nacre quality. In earrings, necklaces and other pieces containing several pearls, their matching is another quality consideration.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: Compare pearls side by side under consistent lighting, rather than comparing photographs taken with different exposure or retouching. Look at the clarity of reflections as well as surface marks. For a strand or pair, assess whether luster is consistent across the whole piece. Do not let a large diameter or a round shape substitute for examining the actual pearl surface.
