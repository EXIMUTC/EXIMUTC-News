De Beers to assume full ownership of the Gahcho Kué diamond mine

October 4, 2026 at 11:37 PM ET

Mountain Province Diamonds says its restructuring agreement provides for De Beers to assume full ownership of Gahcho Kué by acquiring Mountain Province's 49% participating interest.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC's market view: the transaction changes ownership of a producing natural-diamond mine, but it does not by itself establish future rough-diamond prices or the value of any individual diamond.
