Aligned inclusions can create a cat’s-eye effect in gems

November 23, 2026 at 8:04 PM ET

GEMOLOGY FACT: A narrow line can appear when light reflects from aligned needle-like inclusions in a gemstone. A rounded cabochon and the orientation of its base help the effect show.

EXIMUTC Market View

EXIMUTC view: Examine the line’s sharpness and movement in person; a photograph alone should not decide identity or quality.
